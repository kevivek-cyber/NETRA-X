Section OneExecutive Summary

The headline

NETRA-X ranks 3rd–4th of 16 on execution, and 1st on methodology.

No competitor implements dependence discounting — the correction that stops one leak being counted several times. Every scoring approach found (Noisy-OR, weighted linear sums, unstated fusion) assumes evidence is independent. That assumption is wrong in this domain, and it is the single defensible technical claim NETRA-X holds alone.

The gaps against the leaders are engineering, not thinking: enforced RBAC, a pretrained sentence encoder, CI, and a committed Tor testbed. All four are hours-to-a-day of work.

1.2 What the whole field gets wrong

The gap nobody else has closed

Across all 16 repositories, not one mentions handling correlated evidence. The scoring methods found were:

  • Noisy-OR — assumes conditional independence by construction
  • Weighted linear sums — assume independence and additionally saturate
  • Fellegi–Sunter (DRISHTI) — the right framework, but no λ or dependence-group handling described
  • Unstated — the majority

In this domain the assumption is demonstrably false. A reused Bitcoin address and the co-spend cluster containing that address are one operational mistake observed twice. Every competitor counts it twice. NETRA-X counts it once plus a quarter, via λ = 0.25 within a declared dependence group.

3.4 Positioning

If compared to DRISHTI (Fellegi–Sunter)

"We share the framework. The difference is what we added to it. Fellegi–Sunter assumes conditional independence — so a reused wallet and the co-spend cluster containing it count twice. We discount correlated evidence explicitly within dependence groups. And our benchmark reports numbers: ROC-AUC 0.9028, ECE 0.0044, false-attribution rate zero, reproducible at seed 42."

If compared to sharan112212 (Noisy-OR)

"Noisy-OR multiplies out complements, which means enough weak signals push the score toward certainty and the weights themselves are asserted rather than derived. Our m and u are separately defensible — how often you see a clue between the same operator, versus between strangers. And we can go negative: conflicting PGP keys subtract twenty, uncapped, and reject the link outright."

If compared to SENTINEL-X

"Comparable architecture. Ours is auditable end to end — every score decomposes into named evidence items with their discount, their cap and the SHA-256 of the artifact each came from. We also don't claim court admissibility, because admissibility depends on jurisdiction, collection legality and expert testimony, not on a hash chain existing."

The one-line summary

Sixteen teams are scoring evidence. Fifteen of them count the same mistake more than once. That is the argument, and it holds against every implementation found.
